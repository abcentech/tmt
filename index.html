Olubunmi Samuel-Adeyemi
#THEMONEYTHERAPIST · LIMER PERSONAL FINANCE · SINCE 2015

Since 2015, Olubunmi has been facilitating financial literacy and money therapy sessions for business and career professionals across Nigeria and globally. Her overriding passion is to end world poverty — one money conversation at a time.

She has trained over 4,000 young professionals in the art of managing personal and family finances, and authored Hello! My Name is Money: a blind date with money.

SEC Financial Literacy Committee SYLFF Fellow 2021 · Tokyo Foundation PhD Candidate · African Studies

About Olubunmi

Combining financial expertise with the wisdom to listen.

Olubunmi holds a BSc in Mathematical Science, a PGD in Finance from Covenant University, and an MA in African Sustainable Communities. She is a nominated member of the Securities and Exchange Commission's Financial Literacy Technical Committee.

Her work combines rigorous field research with deeply personal therapy sessions — because money is never just about numbers.

"Freedom doesn't come from working harder. It comes from building better systems — and understanding the beliefs that have been running your finances all along."
